To Go

Ukrainian motion verbs can be divided based on 2 dimensions: - whether you go by food/vehicle - whether you are describing a habitual motion or not The main 4 verbs are: 1. By Foot: a. Non-repeated motion: ити or йти (equivalent). b. Repeated motion: ходити 2. By Vehicle: a. Non-repeated motion: їхати b. Repeated motion: їздити

  • There are two equivalent verbs for “to go (on foot)” or “to walk”. These verbs are іти (ite) or йти (“gli”-te).
  • The verb ходити: this is the same verb, but should be used for “to go” in general, as a habit (typically appears with щодня and similar).
  • Imperative:
    • One person: іди! or йди! (go!)
    • Multiple persons: ідіть! or йдіть (let’s go!)

Phrases

  • Я йду до магазину I go to the shop (до = “to”)
  • Куди ти йдеш? where are you going? (right now)
  • іди геть! or ідіть геть go away!
  • іди здоровий or ідіть здорові go healthy! Use as goodbye
  • іди з Богом go with God! Use as goodbye
  • іди з миром go with peace! Use as goodbye
  • іти на небо to go to Heaven. Means “to die”
  • іти на пенсію to retire. Think of “pensiiu” as pension.
    • коли люди в Україні ідуть на пенсію (when do people in Ukraine retire?)
  • іти під ніж (literally “to go under the knife”) means “to have an operation/to be operated”.
  • іти у гості to go as a guest.
  • іти на компроміс to go for a compromise.
  • іти пішки to go by foot.
    • Я йду пішки на роботу (I go to work by foot)
  • іти на прогулянку to go for a walk (think about pro + “huliate” verb)
